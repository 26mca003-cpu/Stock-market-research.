# Problem Statement

## Background

Retail investors researching Indian equities often need information from market-data providers, exchange announcements, annual reports, financial statements, and news feeds. These sources are fragmented and vary in freshness, format, and terminology.

## The Problem

A user can spend significant time manually assembling data before they can compare a company’s business quality, financial strength, valuation, governance, growth, and technical context. Manual workflows also make it easy to overlook pledge disclosures, filing changes, or inconsistent signals.

## Who is Affected

The primary users are Indian retail investors, students, and finance learners who need a repeatable research workflow but do not have institutional research tooling.

## Why It Matters

Fragmented research increases time-to-decision and makes analysis inconsistent. It can also encourage users to rely on headlines or isolated ratios instead of a balanced view of company quality and risk.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Broker dashboards typically focus on quotes and a limited set of ratios, while screening sites, filings portals, and news services remain separate. Vriddhi combines these inputs into one explainable, educational workflow and makes its scoring assumptions visible.
